What I do

Two things, done properly.

Monthly bookkeeping

Ongoing upkeep so you always know where you stand. Transactions categorized, accounts reconciled, statements in your inbox every month.

  • Bank & card reconciliation
  • Transaction categorization
  • Monthly P&L and balance sheet
  • Clean books your tax preparer will thank you for

Catch-up & cleanup

For books that are months — or years — behind. I sort out what happened, fix what's wrong, and hand you back a set of records that reconcile.

  • Back months brought current
  • Miscategorized and duplicate entries fixed
  • Undeposited funds and stale balances resolved
  • Optional handoff into monthly service

Ongoing

Monthly bookkeeping

Your books stay current month to month, so tax season is a non-event and you can answer "how are we doing?" without guessing.

  • All bank, credit card, and loan accounts reconciled
  • Income and expenses categorized consistently
  • Profit & loss and balance sheet delivered monthly
  • Chart of accounts kept sane as the business changes
  • Year-end package ready for your tax preparer
  • Questions answered by me, directly, as they come up

One-time

Catch-up & cleanup

Behind on the books happens to almost everyone. The fix is methodical, not dramatic — and the sooner it starts, the smaller it is.

  • Prior months or years reconstructed and reconciled
  • Duplicates, miscategorizations, and orphan entries cleared
  • Opening balances tied out to real statements
  • QuickBooks file reorganized where it's gone sideways
  • A written summary of what was wrong and what I changed
  • Roll straight into monthly service, or don't — your call

About

Clay Zarada

I'm a bookkeeper in Montgomery, Texas, and a certified QuickBooks ProAdvisor. Zarada Bookkeeping is small on purpose: I take on the number of clients I can personally keep current, which means the person who knows your books is the person who picks up the phone.

Most people find me in one of two states — either they've never had real books, or they had them and fell behind. Neither is a problem. What matters is getting the records honest and then keeping them that way, month after month, so that nothing about your finances is a surprise.

I work remotely with clients anywhere in the country, and I'm glad to meet in person around Conroe and Montgomery when that's easier.
