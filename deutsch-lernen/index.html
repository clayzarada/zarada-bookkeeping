What's inside

Everything you need to study, in one place.

Beginner course

Short lessons from your first "Hallo" to the past tense. Each one has new words, a dialogue, one grammar point and a quiz.

Flashcards that schedule themselves

Spaced repetition shows each card just before you'd forget it. Start from 20 ready-made packs or import your Anki decks.

Grammar you can drill

Two guided courses for declensions and verbs, plus quick drills for der, die, das, plurals, numbers and word order.

Your own textbook

Import a PDF and read it in the app. Look up any word instantly in the built-in dictionary and turn it into a flashcard.

A plan for today

One button builds today's session from what's due, what you got wrong and where you left off, sized to your daily goal.

AI when you want it

With Plus, the app marks your textbook exercises and your writing, writes practice sets from your chapter, and coaches you.

Questions

Good to know.

What do I need to run it?

A Windows or Mac computer. The app runs on your computer and keeps your study data there. The free features work without an internet connection.

Do I need to know any German?

No. A short setup asks your level, and beginners start with lesson one of the built-in course. If you already have a textbook, you can import it and study from that.

What does Plus add?

The AI features: marking the exercises you do in your textbook, marking your writing, practice sets and flashcards made from your chapters, a study coach, and listening episodes. It also backs up your progress so it follows you between computers.

Is there a limit on the AI?

Yes, a monthly fair-use limit. It's set well above what steady daily study uses. If you reach it, the AI features pause until your next month starts, and everything else keeps working.

Can I cancel or get a refund?

You can cancel any time and keep Plus until the end of the period you paid for. If Plus isn't for you, ask within 14 days of a payment and we'll refund it. The refund policy has the details.
