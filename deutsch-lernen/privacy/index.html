Privacy Policy

Effective November 15, 2026

In short: the free app keeps your study data on your own computer and sends us nothing. If you subscribe to Plus, we store your email, your subscription status, how much AI you've used, and your backups if you turn them on. We don't sell your data, show ads or use tracking.

1. Who is responsible

Zarada Software, a division of Zarada Holdings Group LLC, Montgomery, Texas, United States, publishes Deutsch lernen and is responsible for the data described here. Contact: support@deutschlernenapp.com.

2. The free app

The app runs on your computer. Your textbooks, notes, answers, flashcards and progress are stored in a folder on that computer, and we cannot see them. Without an account, the app contacts the internet only in these cases:

  • Update checks. The app asks GitHub whether a newer version exists and downloads it if you choose to update. GitHub sees your IP address, as any website does.
  • AI with your own key. If you enter your own Claude or Gemini API key, AI requests go straight from your computer to Anthropic or Google under your own account with them. They do not pass through us.
  • Sending cards to Anki. This talks only to the Anki program on your own computer.

5. What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent personal data.
  • We don't show ads or use advertising or analytics trackers in the app or on these pages.
  • We don't read your textbooks, notes or backups, except when you ask us for help with a specific problem and give permission.

6. Legal basis (for people in the EU, UK and similar regions)

We process account, subscription and AI request data to perform our contract with you. We process usage and device records because we have a legitimate interest in keeping the service fair and secure. We keep payment records where the law requires it.

7. Your choices and rights

  • See or correct your data, or get a copy: email us.
  • Delete your account and backups: use "Delete account" in the app's settings, or email us. We delete within 30 days, except records we must keep for tax and accounting.
  • Stop using Plus: cancel, and use the app without an account.
  • Complain: if you live in the EU or UK, you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

8. Security

Connections to our servers are encrypted. Access to account data is limited to what each account owner may see, and our own AI keys are kept only on the server. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your data, we will tell you as the law requires.

9. Children

Accounts are for people aged 16 and over. We don't knowingly collect data from younger children. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we will delete it.

10. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will say so in the app or by email before the change applies. The version posted here, with its effective date, is the current one.
