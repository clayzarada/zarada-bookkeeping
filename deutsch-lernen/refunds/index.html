Refund Policy

Effective November 15, 2026

In short: if Plus isn't right for you, ask within 14 days of a payment and you get that payment back in full. You can cancel any time, and the free app stays free.

14-day refund

You can ask for a full refund of any Plus payment within 14 days of the date you were charged. This covers your first payment and each renewal, on monthly plans, yearly plans and one-time passes. You don't need to give a reason.

How to ask

  • Email support@deutschlernenapp.com from the address on your account, or include your order number from the receipt.
  • Or use the link in your Paddle receipt to contact Paddle, our reseller, who handles the payment.

We answer within 3 business days. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method through Paddle. Your bank may take 5 to 10 business days to show it.

After 14 days

Payments older than 14 days are not normally refunded. If something went wrong on our side, for example Plus didn't work for you for a long stretch, or you were charged twice, write to us and we will put it right.

Cancelling

Cancelling stops future charges; it is different from a refund. You can cancel any time from "Manage subscription" in the app or from the link in your receipt. Plus stays active until the end of the period you already paid for, and then your account goes back to the free plan. Your study data stays on your computer either way.

Free trials

If you start with a free trial, you aren't charged until it ends. Cancel before then and you pay nothing. If you forget and are charged, the 14-day refund above applies.

When a refund is made

When we refund a payment, Plus ends for that period and the account returns to the free plan. We may decline repeated refund requests from the same person that look like an attempt to use Plus without paying.

Your legal rights

This policy adds to any rights you have under the consumer laws of your country, such as the 14-day right of withdrawal in the EU and UK. It doesn't replace them.
